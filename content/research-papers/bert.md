Summary notes on bidirectional transformer pretraining.

BERT pretrains deep bidirectional representations by jointly conditioning on left and
right context in all layers, then fine-tunes for downstream tasks.

- Masked language modeling as the pretraining objective
- Strong results across many NLP benchmarks with minimal task-specific changes
