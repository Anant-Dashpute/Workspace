Summary notes on the Transformer architecture paper.

Introduces the Transformer, an architecture based entirely on attention mechanisms,
removing recurrence and convolutions entirely.

- Self-attention lets the model weigh all positions in a sequence directly
- Enabled the current generation of large language models
