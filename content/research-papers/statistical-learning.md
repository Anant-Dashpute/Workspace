Summary notes on the statistical foundations underlying modern ML methods.

Covers the bias-variance tradeoff, model selection, and regularization as core
statistical tools for building predictive models that generalize well beyond
training data.

- Bias-variance tradeoff explains overfitting and underfitting in model selection
- Regularization (ridge, lasso) constrains model complexity to improve generalization
- Cross-validation and bootstrap provide practical estimates of test error
