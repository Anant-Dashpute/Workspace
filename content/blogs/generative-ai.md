Exploring how generative models moved beyond text into images, audio, and video.

Generative AI, powered by models like GPT and diffusion-based image generators, is
reshaping how we create content. These models generate realistic text, images, and
video, pushing the boundaries of creativity and automation.

- Applications span design, entertainment, and software development
- Raises open questions about originality and attribution
