Associated with K.K. Wagh Institute of Engineering Education & Research (Sep 2017 – Mar 2018).
Contributors: Pranali and Ruchira.

Created an OFDM transceiver flow graph covering different modulation schemes on an
FPGA-based SDR kit, achieving large bandwidth coverage with different antennas to
receive FM radio signals and transmit audio.

- Implemented modulation schemes: ASK, BPSK, QPSK, QAM-16, QAM-256
- Built the transceiver flow graph on an FPGA-based Software Defined Radio kit
- Used different antennas to receive FM radio signals and transmit audio over large bandwidth
